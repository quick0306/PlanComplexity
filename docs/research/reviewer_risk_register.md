# Plan_Complexity_Metrics Reviewer Risk Register

> Imported on 2026-08-11 from the legacy unified-workspace snapshot.
> This document records research framing and evidence boundaries; it is not completed clinical evidence.

## Reviewer Risks

- System-specific metrics may be compared too broadly.
- Validation fixtures may not cover all delivery systems.
- Shared-code extraction may blur project-specific assumptions.

## Required Inputs

- Metric definitions, validation tests, system-specific assumptions, and extraction registry notes.

## Evidence Boundary

Do not resolve risks by collapsing distinct delivery-system concepts into one generic complexity label.

## Traceability

Track each risk to source definitions, tests, manuscript text, and response status.
